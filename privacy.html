Alai · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Alai (அலை, formerly RadioTamil – Live Tamil FM) is a Tamil radio and podcast app for Android phones, Android Auto and Android Automotive, published by Corneon AI. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who it is shared with, and your choices.

1) Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise (GPS) location.
  • Your favourites, history, recordings, downloads and alarms stay on your device.
  • We use Google services for analytics, crash reports, notifications, ads and purchases.
  • We estimate your country/city from your IP address to suggest nearby stations.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2) Information the app uses

  • Approximate location from your IP address. To show "Near you" stations and the world map, the app sends a request to ipwho.is (or ipapi.co as a fallback), which return an approximate country, region and city for your IP address. We do not use GPS and do not store this on our servers.
  • App activity and diagnostics (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring): screens opened, features used, stations played, crash logs, device model, Android version and app version.
  • Advertising ID (Google AdMob), used to show and measure ads to listeners without Premium.
  • Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), if you allow notifications.
  • Purchase status. Alai Premium is bought through Google Play. We receive whether you have an active purchase; we never receive your card or payment details.
  • App settings from our servers (Firebase Remote Config), for example feature switches and limits.

3) Information kept only on your device

  • Favourites, recently played stations, song history and recent searches.
  • Recordings you make of a stream, podcast episodes you download, alarms and sleep timers.
  • Your theme, accent colour and language.

These are not uploaded by Alai. They are removed when you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it.

4) Services that receive requests from the app

  • Radio stations: audio is streamed directly from each station's own server, which sees your IP address.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): the public station directory.
  • Apple iTunes Search API: cover art for the song playing, and podcast search. The artist and title being played may be sent to look up artwork.
  • ipwho.is / ipapi.co: approximate location from IP (see section 2).
  • Google: Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Installations), AdMob and its consent tool (UMP), and Google Play Billing.
  • Chromecast / Google Cast: if you cast, the app looks for devices on your local network.

Each service handles data under its own privacy policy, for example Google's at policies.google.com/privacy.

5) Ads and consent

Listeners without Premium may see ads from Google AdMob (a card in the Home feed, a full-screen ad between stations, and a muted video on Now Playing). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown; you can change it any time in Settings › Privacy choices. The Kids section shows no ads. Alai Premium removes ads.

6) Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to stream radio and podcasts.
  • Foreground service, wake lock: to keep playing with the screen off.
  • Notifications: playback controls, alarms and occasional reminders (optional).
  • Alarms and full-screen alerts, start at boot: so radio alarms ring on time, even on a locked phone.
  • Wi-Fi multicast: to find Cast devices on your network.

Alai does not use the microphone; recordings are copies of the stream, not of your surroundings.

7) How we use information

  • To play stations and podcasts, and suggest stations near you.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve speed and reliability.
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve them.
  • To show ads to listeners without Premium, and to provide Premium to those who buy it.

8) Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the services listed in section 4, to run the features described, or when required by law.

9) Retention

Analytics, crash and performance data are kept by Google under the retention settings of our Firebase project and Google's own policies. We keep no separate copy on our servers.

10) Your choices

  • Turn notifications off in Settings › Notifications.
  • Change ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices (where shown), and reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Delete favourites, history, recordings and downloads in the app, or clear the app's storage.
  • Ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app installation: email us (section 13).

11) Children

Alai is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Its Kids section offers stories, rhymes and Thirukkural for families to enjoy together, shows no ads, and asks for no personal information.

12) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
